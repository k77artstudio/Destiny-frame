The Core Experience

The K77 Chain —
Where Art
Comes Alive.

Hidden within every framed K77 artwork is a discreet QR code. Scan it and the frame opens into something no gallery, no auction house and no other artist has ever offered. Three private layers. One living archive.

I

Layer One

The Voice
of the Artist

Every piece carries a spoken message tied directly to the artwork, recorded by the artist and never repeated. It could be poetry read quietly over soft piano, a dramatic narration of what the typography holds, or for commissioned works, a deeply personal message about the story behind what was created. You do not just hang the art. You hear it.

II

Layer Two

The Living
Artwork

The original piece is presented digitally, not as a static image but as a breathing moving version of itself. Subtle motion. Its title revealed. An immersive viewing experience that transforms your physical frame into a portal. The art does not stay still. It never did.

III

Layer Three

The Ownership
Record

A private digital certificate built for permanence. Your name, the year of collection and a unique artwork ID that belongs to this piece and no other. A full ownership history tracking every collector who has ever held this work. When you resell, your name is not erased. It becomes part of the art's legacy.
